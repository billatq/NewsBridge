# NewsBridge

NewsBridge is a small iOS app with standard share and action extensions that lets you open articles in Apple News. The installed app is named **News Share**.

It exists because if you want to go from say, a news article in an RSS feed reader directly to Apple News, you have to first hop over to Safari, and then click again to get to Apple News. This isn't a problem on the desktop version of Apple News, but weirdly is on iOS.

## Disclaimer

This is an unofficial interoperability project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Apple Inc. Apple News is a trademark of Apple Inc.

The bridge relies on undocumented private iOS behavior. It may stop working at any point.
